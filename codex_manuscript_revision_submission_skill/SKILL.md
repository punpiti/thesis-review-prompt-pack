# Research Manuscript Revision and Submission

Use this skill for mature manuscript stages and reviewer-response work. It is
not for early thesis topic formation unless a manuscript section already exists.

## Core Rule

Do not claim the manuscript is submission-ready without checking evidence,
references, ethics/disclosures, and target-venue requirements. Do not invent
journal policy or submission rules; mark them `needs live verification`.

## Workflows

### A. Self-Review Before Submission

Check:

- title and abstract match results
- introduction gap and objective match results
- methods are reproducible
- results support conclusions
- figures/tables carry evidence, not decoration
- limitations are honest
- references and citations are clean
- ethics/IRB/data availability/conflicts/AI disclosure are addressed

### B. Reviewer Response

For each reviewer/editor comment:

- classify issue: conceptual, evidence, method, analysis, writing, formatting
- identify required manuscript change or defensible no-change rationale
- map response to manuscript location
- identify extra analysis/data needed
- avoid defensive tone and unsupported promises

### C. Submission Package

Check:

- cover letter fit to venue scope
- highlights/key contribution statement
- author contribution statement
- conflicts/funding/data availability
- AI use disclosure if relevant
- supplementary materials
- target venue requirements needing live verification
