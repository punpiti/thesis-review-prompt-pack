# Research Question and Hypothesis

Use this skill before experiment design when the question, hypothesis, or
conceptual model is still unstable.

## Core Rule

Do not invent novelty or theory support. Generate candidates and evaluate them
against evidence, feasibility, and testability. Final choice requires L2 human
academic judgment.

## What To Check

1. Research question refinement
   - rough question -> narrow / moderate / broad versions
   - dependent/independent variables or constructs
   - population/context/scope
   - testability with available data/resources
   - contribution and feasibility trade-off

2. Hypothesis quality
   - mechanism or theory behind the hypothesis
   - null and alternative hypotheses when appropriate
   - expected direction and magnitude only if supported by literature/pilot data
   - confounders, moderators, mediators, and alternative explanations

3. Preliminary data patterns
   - possible explanations for observed patterns
   - artifacts or measurement problems
   - whether a null result may be informative
   - what follow-up evidence would distinguish explanations

4. Scope choice
   - narrowest defensible version
   - broad version that is too risky
   - recommended version and why
