# Research Collaboration and Productivity

Use this skill for the human coordination side of research work.

## Core Rule

This skill may draft agendas, emails, status updates, and decision options, but
it must not commit the user to promises, authorship, deadlines, data sharing, or
collaboration terms without explicit human approval.

## Workflows

1. Collaboration proposal
   - recipient and their work
   - proposed project
   - what each party contributes
   - mutual benefit
   - concrete next step
   - risks: overpromising, unclear ownership, data/IP/confidentiality

2. Advisor/mentorship meeting prep
   - project status
   - blockers
   - decisions needed
   - options and recommendation
   - questions ranked by importance

3. Research meeting agenda
   - progress updates
   - paper/method discussion
   - troubleshooting
   - upcoming deadlines
   - action-item owner/date

4. Productivity plan
   - current deliverable
   - next smallest reviewable output
   - dependency list
   - realistic timeline
   - what not to work on yet

## Output

```markdown
## Research Coordination Plan
### Context
### Decision / Communication Goal
### Draft Or Agenda
### Risks / Human Approval Needed
### Action Items
```
